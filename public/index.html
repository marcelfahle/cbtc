The mountains are the main event

We run, we climb, we spend a few good hours outside. Then we come back to good food and time to recover. No packed workshop schedule. Just four days with room to breathe.

Your kind of camp?

You already run.
You’re curious what’s next.

You already have some experience on trails. You don’t need to be fast or an ultra runner.

Be comfortable spending several hours moving in the mountains: climbs, rocky descents and hiking the steep sections. Expect around 2–4 hours on the trails each day.

This is for women who want more trail time and a few days away from everyday life.

A day in the mountains

Out together. Back to yourself.

  1. 01 /

    Morning

    Breakfast and coffee together. Check the weather, pack for the day and head out.

  2. 02 /

    Trail

    2–4 hours on the trails. Climbs, rocky descents, hiking the steep bits and stops for the view.

  3. 03 /

    Recover

    A warm shower, food and time to rest. Sauna and spa are part of the plan; final inclusions to come.

  4. 04 /

    Evening

    Dinner and conversation. Or a little quiet before we head out again tomorrow.

Winter has its own plans

Some days are blue sky.
Some days are wet shoes.

Clouds, wind and rain are part of winter in the mountains. We adapt to the conditions: change the route, shorten the day or run in the rain when it’s safe.

Safety comes before any itinerary. Exact routes will be confirmed with the winter dates.
