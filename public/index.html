What is it?

Four days of proper trail running in the Costa Blanca mountains, wrapped inside a beautiful winter escape.

Some days may be sunny and mild. Another day may bring clouds, wind or rain. It’s winter in the mountains. You never quite know.

We run. We climb. We spend a few good hours outside. Then we come back.

Warm shower. Good food. Sauna. Time to recover. Dinner together. And the next morning, we head back out.

There is no packed schedule of workshops and no classroom feeling.

The mountains are the main event. Everything around them is there to make those days even better.

Who is it for?

You already run.
You already have some experience on trails.
And spending a few hours in the mountains sounds exciting to you.

You don’t need to be fast. You don’t need to be an ultra runner.

But you should already know what trail running feels like — climbs, descents, rocky terrain, hiking the steep sections — and be comfortable spending several hours moving in the mountains.

This camp is for women who want more trail time, more confidence and a few days away from everyday life.

What your days look like

Morning

Breakfast together. Coffee. A relaxed start.

We talk through the day, check the weather, pack what we need and head out.

Mountains

Around 2–4 hours on the trails, depending on the day.

Climbs. Technical sections. Rocky descents. Running. Hiking. Food stops. Long views.

The kind of day where your legs are tired, your head is quiet, and you come back feeling like you have really been somewhere.

Afternoon

Food. Shower. Sauna. Spa. Massage if you want one. Time to rest.

A few hours where there is nothing you need to organise and nowhere else you need to be.

Just recovery, quiet, good food and the feeling of having earned it.

Evening

Dinner together.

Good food, tired legs, conversation. Simple.

What do we learn?

We are not building this camp around formal lessons.

We run alongside you and share what we have learned from years of training and racing in the mountains.

That naturally includes things like:

  • pacing long climbs
  • moving over technical terrain
  • descending with more confidence
  • fuelling on longer days
  • managing effort
  • knowing when to run and when to hike

No lectures. Just experience, conversation and time together in the mountains.

What if it rains?

Then we adapt.

We may change the route, shorten the day, or simply run in the rain.

Winter on the Costa Blanca can bring beautiful sunny days, but also clouds, wind and wet trails. That is part of the mountains.

The good news is that winters here are generally mild, especially compared with northern Europe.

And coming back to a warm hotel, sauna and dinner after a wet day outside has its own kind of charm.

Run outside. Recover properly.

A small boutique hotel in the Mediterranean countryside, surrounded by olive trees, mountains and quiet.

In winter, the landscape has its own character — green valleys, soft light, olive groves and, if the timing is right, almond trees starting to bloom. You’ll see plenty of it on the trails too.

Good food. Sauna and spa. Space to rest. Somewhere warm and beautiful to come back to.

A small group — on purpose.

We are not trying to fill as many beds as possible. We want to create the right group.

Fitness matters. Trail experience matters. But personality matters too.

With a small group, the women around you become a big part of the experience — on the trail, around the dinner table and during the quieter moments in between.

That is why everyone applies and meets us before being offered a place.

We choose the group based on fitness, trail experience, expectations and overall group fit.

Not because we want the camp to feel exclusive. Because we care about what these days feel like — and about the women you share them with.

We want you to go home with tired legs, a clear head, good memories and the feeling that those few days gave you something back.

How it works

  1. 1. Join the waitlist

    Be the first to receive the winter dates.

  2. 2. Apply

    Tell us a little about yourself, your running and your trail experience.

  3. 3. Meet us

    Once the group is confirmed, we’ll bring everyone together for a relaxed online group session before the camp.

    So when you arrive, you’re not walking into a room full of complete strangers.
