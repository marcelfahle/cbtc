A camp day

A climb. A view. No rush to be anywhere else

Mountain days, with time to enjoy the hours between them. A taste of the camp, not a fixed timetable.

  • MorningCoffee first

    Check the weather, pack your bag and head for the trails.

  • TrailUp you go

    Climb, run, hike the steep bits. Stop for the view without apologising for it.

  • RecoverShoes off

    Feet up. Time to let your legs catch up with you.

  • EveningYour call

    Stay for the conversation or take some quiet time. Both count as a good evening.

Whatever the weather

Rain makes the coffee taste better

We don’t make the rules. Some days bring blue skies. Others bring wind, wet shoes and an even better reason to warm up afterwards.

We adapt the route to the weather, shorten the day when needed and only head out when it’s safe. The mountains get the final say.

Before you pack your trail shoes

Get the details. Meet us. Then decide

For now, just tell us which camps could work. No payment. No commitment.

  1. Get the details

    We’ll email the dates and details for the camps you choose.

  2. Tell us about your running

    When applications open, share your trail experience and what you want from the camp.

  3. Meet the hosts

    Ask the questions that matter to you before deciding. Meetings open with applications.

  4. Find your group

    We consider trail experience, fitness and expectations before offering a place.

  5. Make it yours

    Once you’ve been offered a place and want to join, a deposit secures it. No payments before the required registration and insurance are in place.

  6. Meet the women you’ll run with

    An online catch-up before camp to talk travel, gear and the days ahead.
