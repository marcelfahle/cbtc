A January day

A climb. A view. No rush to be anywhere else

Four mountain days, with time to enjoy the hours between them. A taste of January, not a fixed timetable.

  • 01

    Morning

    Coffee first. Check the weather, pack your bag and head for the trails.

  • 02

    Trail

    Climb, run, hike the steep bits. Stop for the view without apologising for it. Expect around 2–4 hours outside.

  • 03

    Recover

    Shoes off. Sauna on. Time to let your legs catch up with you.

  • 04

    Evening

    Stay for the conversation or take some quiet time. Both count as a good evening.

January, whatever the weather

Rain makes the sauna better

We don’t make the rules. Some days bring blue skies. Others bring wind, wet shoes and an even better reason to warm up afterwards.

We adapt the route to the weather, shorten the day when needed and only head out when it’s safe. The mountains get the final say.
